Buttermilk Waffles

Crisp-edged, fluffy buttermilk waffles with a light batter that’s mixed in minutes. Freeze extras and toast them for busy mornings.

Instructions

  1. Heat the waffle iron. Heat the oven to 200°F and set a wire rack on a baking sheet inside to keep waffles crisp.Heat the waffle iron. Heat the oven to 95°C and set a wire rack on a baking sheet inside to keep waffles crisp.

  2. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt in a large bowl.

  3. In another bowl whisk the buttermilk, eggs, melted butter and vanilla. Pour into the flour mixture and stir until just combined; a few lumps are fine. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes.

  4. Brush the iron lightly with oil. Pour in enough batter to cover about two-thirds of the grid (it spreads) and close the lid.

  5. Cook until the waffle is deep golden and the steam slows to a trickle, usually 4–5 minutes depending on your iron. Move it to the rack in the oven and repeat with the rest of the batter.

Notes

  • Don’t stack hot waffles; they steam each other soft. The rack keeps them crisp.
  • Freeze cooled waffles in a single layer, then bag them. Reheat straight from frozen in a toaster.
