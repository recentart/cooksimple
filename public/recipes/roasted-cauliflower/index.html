Spiced Roasted Cauliflower

Cauliflower florets roasted until golden and nutty with cumin, turmeric and garlic, finished with lemon and herbs.

Servings

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the cauliflower with the oil, cumin, turmeric, garlic powder and salt on the baking sheet.Heat the oven to 220°C. Toss the cauliflower with the oil, cumin, turmeric, garlic powder and salt on the baking sheet.

  2. Spread it in a single layer and roast, stirring once, until tender and deeply golden at the edges, 25–30 minutes.

  3. Toss with the lemon juice and herbs and serve. A drizzle of tahini sauce is good on top.

Notes

  • Turmeric stains; use a wooden spoon you don’t mind turning yellow.
