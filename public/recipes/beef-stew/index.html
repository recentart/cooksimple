Beef Stew

Tender chunks of beef slowly braised with red wine, carrots, potatoes and thyme in a rich, thick gravy. Worth every minute.

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 325°F. Pat the beef dry, season with 1 tsp of the salt and the pepper, and toss with the flour.Heat the oven to 165°C. Pat the beef dry, season with 1 tsp of the salt and the pepper, and toss with the flour.

  2. Heat 2 tbsp oil in the pot over medium-high heat. Brown the beef in batches, without crowding, until well browned on several sides, 5–6 minutes per batch. Move to a plate.

  3. Add the rest of the oil and the onion and cook until soft, 5 minutes, scraping up the browned bits. Add the garlic and tomato paste and stir for 1 minute.

  4. Pour in the wine and simmer for 2 minutes. Add the broth, Worcestershire, thyme, bay leaves, the remaining salt and the beef with its juices. Bring to a simmer, cover and move to the oven for 1½ hours.

  5. Stir in the carrots and potatoes, cover and return to the oven until the beef and vegetables are fork-tender, 45–60 minutes.

  6. Remove the bay leaves, stir in the peas and let them warm through for 5 minutes. Taste for salt and serve.

Notes

  • Chuck is the right cut: its connective tissue melts into tender, juicy meat. Lean cuts turn dry.
  • Like most stews, it’s better the next day. Keeps 4 days, or freeze (without potatoes, which go grainy) for 3 months.
