Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

Spaghetti tossed with olive oil, gently toasted sliced garlic and red pepper flakes, with starchy pasta water pulling it into a glossy sauce.

Servings
4
Prep
10 min
Cook
20 min
Total
30 min
  • Dinner
  • Quick
  • Vegan
  • Italian

Ingredients

Servings
Units

A 12-inch skillet holds about 1 pound of spaghetti. For a bigger batch, cook the garlic and toss the pasta in a wide Dutch oven instead.

  • water stays the same: A big pot of water cooks any batch size, so it stays the same.
  • kosher salt stays the same: Seasons the pot of water, which stays the same size at any batch.

Equipment

  • Large pot
  • 12-inch skillet30 cm skillet
  • Tongs

Instructions

  1. Bring the water to a boil in a large covered pot, then uncover it and stir in the salt.

  2. Add the spaghetti and cook, stirring now and then, until it bends easily but is still firm in the center, 7–8 minutes, or about 2 minutes short of the package time. It finishes cooking in the sauce.

  3. While the pasta cooks, put the oil and garlic in a 12-inch skillet over medium-low heat. Cook, stirring often, until the garlic is pale gold and fragrant, 4–6 minutes. It keeps darkening off the heat, so stop at pale gold rather than brown.While the pasta cooks, put the oil and garlic in a 30 cm skillet over medium-low heat.   4. Take the skillet off the heat and stir in the red pepper flakes. Scoop out 1 cup of the pasta water, then drain the spaghetti and add it to the skillet with ½ cup of the reserved water.Take the skillet off the heat and stir in the red pepper flakes.   5. Set the skillet over medium-high heat and toss constantly with tongs until the water and oil come together into a light, glossy sauce that clings to the pasta, 2–3 minutes. Add more pasta water a splash at a time if it looks dry or oily.

  6. Take the pan off the heat and toss in the parsley. Taste, add a pinch of salt if needed and serve right away, because the sauce tightens as it cools.

Notes

  • Slice the garlic as thin and even as you can so it all turns golden at the same time. Thick slices stay pale while thin ones burn.
  • If you're not cooking vegan, a little grated Parmesan or pecorino over each plate is a good addition.
  • Best eaten right away. Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 3 days; warm them in a skillet with a splash of water.
