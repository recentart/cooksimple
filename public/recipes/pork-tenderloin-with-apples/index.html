Pork Tenderloin with Apples

Juicy roasted pork tenderloin with caramelized apples and onions in a quick cider-mustard pan sauce, all in one skillet.

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Trim any silvery membrane from the pork and season it all over with the salt, pepper and thyme.Heat the oven to 220°C. Trim any silvery membrane from the pork and season it all over with the salt, pepper and thyme.

  2. Heat the oil in the skillet over medium-high heat and brown the pork on all sides, 6–8 minutes total. Move it to a plate.

  3. Add the butter, apples and onion and cook, turning, until lightly caramelized, 4–5 minutes. Set the pork on top.

  4. Roast until the thickest part of the pork reads 140°F, 12–15 minutes. Move the pork to a board to rest for 5 minutes; it will climb to 145°F.Roast until the thickest part of the pork reads 60°C, 12–15 minutes. Move the pork to a board to rest for 5 minutes; it will climb to 63°C.

  5. Set the skillet over medium heat (the handle is hot). Add the cider, vinegar and mustard and simmer, scraping up the browned bits, until slightly syrupy, 3 minutes. Slice the pork and serve with the apples and sauce.

Notes

  • Tenderloin is lean, so a thermometer makes all the difference. Slightly pink pork at 145°F is safe and juicy.
