Tuna Salad Sandwiches

Classic tuna salad with crunchy celery, red onion, lemon and a little mayonnaise, ready in ten minutes with no cooking.

Makes

Instructions

  1. Flake the drained tuna into a bowl with a fork.

  2. Add the mayonnaise, celery, red onion, lemon juice, mustard and pepper and stir until evenly mixed. Taste, then add salt only if needed; canned tuna is already salted.

  3. Toast the bread if you like. Build the sandwiches with lettuce, tomato and a thick layer of tuna salad.

Notes

  • Tuna salad keeps, covered, in the fridge for up to 3 days.
  • Swap half the mayonnaise for plain Greek yogurt for a lighter, tangier salad.
