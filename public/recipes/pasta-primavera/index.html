Pasta Primavera

A bright spring pasta with asparagus, peas, zucchini and cherry tomatoes in a light lemon, garlic and Parmesan sauce.

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in a big pot of salted boiling water. Add the asparagus and peas for the last 2 minutes. Save 1 cup of the water, then drain everything together.Cook the pasta in a big pot of salted boiling water. Add the asparagus and peas for the last 2 minutes. Save 237 mL of the water, then drain everything together.

  2. Meanwhile heat the oil in the skillet over medium-high heat and cook the zucchini until lightly browned, 4 minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute.

  3. Add the cherry tomatoes and cook until they start to soften, 2 minutes.

  4. Add the pasta and vegetables, butter, lemon zest and juice, salt, cheese and a splash of pasta water and toss until glossy. Scatter with basil and serve.

Notes

  • Use whatever green vegetables are in season: broccoli, green beans or snap peas all work.
