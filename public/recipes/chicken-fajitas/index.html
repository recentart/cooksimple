Chicken Fajitas

Smoky, lime-marinated chicken seared hot with peppers and onions, served in warm tortillas with your favorite toppings.

Servings

Prep

Equipment

  • Large cast-iron skillet

Instructions

  1. Toss the chicken with the lime juice, 1 tbsp of the oil, the chili powder, cumin, paprika, garlic powder and salt. Let it sit for 15 minutes while you slice the vegetables.

  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in the skillet over high heat until smoking. Add the peppers and onion and cook, stirring only now and then, until charred in spots but still a little crisp, 5–6 minutes. Move to a plate.

  3. Add the rest of the oil and the chicken in a single layer. Sear without moving for 2 minutes, then stir and cook until browned and cooked through, 3–4 minutes more.

  4. Return the vegetables to the pan and toss together for 1 minute.

  5. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan or wrapped in foil in the oven. Serve the fajitas straight from the skillet with the tortillas, sour cream, salsa and avocado.

Notes

  • A very hot pan is what gives fajitas their char; don’t crowd it, or they’ll steam. Cook in two batches if needed.
