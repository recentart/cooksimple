Egg Salad Sandwiches

Creamy egg salad with celery, chives and a little Dijon, piled into soft bread with lettuce. Simple, quick and good for lunchboxes.

Makes
4 sandwiches
Prep
10 min
Cook
12 min
Total
35 min

Instructions

  1. Put the eggs in the saucepan, cover with cold water by 1-inch and bring to a boil. Turn off the heat, cover and leave for 11 minutes.Put the eggs in the saucepan, cover with cold water by 2.5 cm and bring to a boil. Turn off the heat, cover and leave for 11 minutes.

  2. Drain and cover the eggs with cold water or ice water until cool enough to handle, 5 minutes. Peel them.

  3. Chop the eggs roughly, or mash them with a fork for a smoother salad, in a medium bowl.

  4. Stir in the mayonnaise, mustard, celery, chives, salt and pepper. Taste and add more salt if needed.

  5. Layer lettuce on half the bread slices, spoon the egg salad on top and close the sandwiches.

Notes

  • The egg salad keeps in a covered container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Assemble sandwiches just before eating so the bread stays firm.
