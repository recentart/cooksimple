Classic Deviled Eggs

Hard-cooked egg whites filled with a smooth, tangy yolk mixture and finished with paprika and chives.

Makes
12 halves

Cook

Equipment

  • Saucepan
  • Bowl of ice water
  • Piping bag or zip-top bag, optional

Instructions

  1. Lower the eggs into a pan of boiling water, reduce to a simmer and cook for 12 minutes. Move them straight into the ice water for 10 minutes.

  2. Peel the eggs, halve them lengthwise and pop the yolks into a bowl. Set the whites on a plate.

  3. Mash the yolks until fine, then stir in the mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar, salt and pepper until smooth.

  4. Spoon or pipe the filling into the whites. Dust with paprika and scatter with chives.

Notes

  • Older eggs peel more easily than very fresh ones.
  • Make the filling and whites up to a day ahead and keep them separately covered; fill just before serving.
