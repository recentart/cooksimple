One-Bowl Chocolate Cake

A dark, moist chocolate sheet cake mixed in one bowl, with a glossy chocolate frosting. Reliable for birthdays and bake sales.

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease the pan and line the bottom with parchment.Heat the oven to 175°C. Grease the pan and line the bottom with parchment.

  2. Whisk the flour, sugar, cocoa, baking soda, baking powder and salt in a large bowl. Add the eggs, buttermilk, oil and vanilla and whisk until smooth.

  3. Whisk in the hot coffee; the batter will be thin. That’s what makes the cake so moist. Pour into the pan.

  4. Bake until a toothpick in the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs, 32–36 minutes. Cool completely in the pan, about 1 hour.

  5. Beat the butter and cocoa until smooth, then beat in the powdered sugar, milk and vanilla until fluffy. Spread over the cooled cake.

Notes

  • The coffee deepens the chocolate flavor without tasting of coffee. Hot water works too.
