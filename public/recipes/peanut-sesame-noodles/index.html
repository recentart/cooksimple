Peanut Sesame Noodles

Cool noodles tossed in a creamy peanut, soy and sesame sauce with crisp cucumber, carrot and green onions. Served cold or at room temperature.

Instructions

  1. Bring the water to a boil in a large pot. While it heats, whisk the peanut butter, soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil, maple syrup, garlic, ginger and sriracha in a large bowl until smooth. The sauce will be thick.

  2. Add the spaghetti and cook, stirring often at first, until fully tender, 9–11 minutes. Cook it a little past al dente, because the noodles firm up as they cool.   3. Whisk ¼ cup of the hot cooking water into the sauce until it is smooth and pourable, like heavy cream. Drain the noodles and rinse them under cold running water until cool, then shake off as much water as you can.Whisk 59 mL of the hot cooking water into the sauce until it is smooth and pourable, like heavy cream.   4. Add the noodles, cucumber, carrots and most of the green onions to the sauce. Toss with tongs until every strand is coated, loosening with a little more of the reserved water if the sauce seems thick.

  5. Pile into bowls and top with the remaining green onions, the sesame seeds and the peanuts. Serve cold or at room temperature, with more sriracha on the side.

Notes

  • The cooking water isn't salted because the soy sauce seasons the noodles.
  • Rice noodles work in place of spaghetti. Cook them as the package directs, then rinse and drain the same way.
  • For colder noodles, chill the dressed noodles for 30 minutes and add the cucumber just before serving so it stays crisp.
  • Keeps in the fridge for up to 3 days. The noodles soak up the sauce, so loosen leftovers with a splash of water and a little soy sauce.
