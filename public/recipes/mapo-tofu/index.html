Mapo Tofu

Soft tofu in a fiery, savory Sichuan sauce of chili bean paste, a little ground pork, garlic and tingling Sichuan pepper.

Instructions

  1. Slide the tofu into a pot of gently simmering salted water for 2 minutes; this firms it so it holds together. Drain carefully.

  2. Heat the oil in the wok over medium-high heat. Stir-fry the pork until browned and crisp at the edges, 3–4 minutes.

  3. Lower the heat to medium, add the chili bean paste and fry until the oil turns red, 1 minute. Add the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.

  4. Add the broth, soy sauce and sugar and bring to a simmer. Slide in the tofu and simmer gently, nudging rather than stirring, for 3–4 minutes.

  5. Stir the cornstarch with 2 tbsp water, pour it in and swirl the pan until the sauce thickens and turns glossy, 1 minute. Sprinkle with Sichuan pepper and green onions and serve with rice.Stir the cornstarch with 2 tbsp (30 mL) water, pour it in and swirl the pan until the sauce thickens and turns glossy, 1 minute. Notes

  • For a vegetarian version, replace the pork with finely chopped mushrooms and use vegetable broth.
