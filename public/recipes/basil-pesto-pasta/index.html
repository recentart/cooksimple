Basil Pesto Pasta

Homemade basil pesto with pine nuts, garlic, Parmesan and olive oil, tossed with pasta and a splash of the cooking water.

Equipment

  • Food processor or blender
  • Large pot

Instructions

  1. Bring the water and salt to a boil for the pasta.

  2. Pulse the basil, pine nuts, garlic, cheese and salt in the food processor until finely chopped. With the machine running, pour in the oil until the pesto is fairly smooth. Stir in the lemon juice.

  3. Cook the pasta until al dente. Save 1 cup of the water, then drain.Cook the pasta until al dente. Save 237 mL of the water, then drain.

  4. Toss the pasta with the pesto and enough pasta water to make a glossy sauce that clings. Serve with extra cheese.

Notes

  • Keep pesto green by pressing plastic wrap onto its surface. It keeps 5 days in the fridge or freezes for 3 months in ice-cube trays.
  • Walnuts or sunflower seeds can stand in for pine nuts.
