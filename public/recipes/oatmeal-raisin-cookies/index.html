Oatmeal Raisin Cookies

Chewy oatmeal cookies with plump raisins, brown sugar and a hint of cinnamon, with crisp edges and soft, thick centers.

Instructions

  1. Cover the raisins with hot water for 10 minutes to plump them, then drain and pat dry. Heat the oven to 350°F and line the baking sheets.Cover the raisins with hot water for 10 minutes to plump them, then drain and pat dry. Heat the oven to 175°C and line the baking sheets.

  2. Whisk the flour, baking soda, cinnamon and salt in a bowl.

  3. Beat the butter with both sugars until creamy, 2 minutes. Beat in the eggs and vanilla.

  4. Mix in the flour mixture, then the oats and raisins, until evenly combined.

  5. Scoop rounded 2-tablespoon balls onto the sheets with a 2-inch gap between them. Bake one sheet at a time until the edges are golden but the centers still look soft, 11–13 minutes.Scoop rounded 2-tablespoon balls onto the sheets with a 5 cm gap between them. Bake one sheet at a time until the edges are golden but the centers still look soft, 11–13 minutes.

  6. Cool on the sheet for 5 minutes before moving to a rack; they firm up as they cool.

Notes

  • Chill the dough for 30 minutes for thicker cookies that spread less.
  • Keeps 5 days in an airtight tin.
