Carrot Cake with Cream Cheese Frosting

A moist, warmly spiced carrot cake with walnuts, baked in one pan and topped with a thick layer of tangy cream cheese frosting.

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease and line the pan. Whisk the flour, baking soda, baking powder, cinnamon, nutmeg and salt.Heat the oven to 175°C and grease and line the pan. Whisk the flour, baking soda, baking powder, cinnamon, nutmeg and salt.

  2. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs with both sugars until smooth, then whisk in the oil and vanilla.

  3. Stir in the flour mixture until just combined, then fold in the carrots and walnuts. Spread in the pan.

  4. Bake until the top springs back and a toothpick in the center comes out clean, 35–40 minutes. Cool completely in the pan, at least 1 hour.

  5. Beat the cream cheese and butter until smooth, then beat in the powdered sugar and vanilla until fluffy. Spread over the cooled cake.

Notes

  • Grate the carrots finely; coarse shreds make a drier, patchy cake.
  • Store the frosted cake in the fridge for up to 5 days; let slices sit out 20 minutes before serving.
