Soft Dinner Rolls

Pillowy, buttery pull-apart dinner rolls made with instant yeast and brushed with melted butter as they come out of the oven.

Instructions

  1. Stir the warm milk, yeast and sugar together in a large bowl. Add the egg, softened butter, flour and salt and mix into a soft dough.

  2. Knead by machine on medium-low or by hand until smooth, elastic and just slightly tacky, 6–8 minutes. Add flour a spoonful at a time only if it’s very sticky.

  3. Cover and let rise in a warm place until doubled, 1 hour.

  4. Butter the pan. Divide the dough into 12 pieces, roll each into a tight ball and set them in the pan in rows. Cover and let rise until puffy and touching, 30–40 minutes. Heat the oven to 375°F.Butter the pan. Divide the dough into 12 pieces, roll each into a tight ball and set them in the pan in rows. Cover and let rise until puffy and touching, 30–40 minutes.   5. Bake until golden brown and the center rolls read 190°F, 18–22 minutes. Brush with the melted butter right away.Bake until golden brown and the center rolls read 88°C, 18–22 minutes. Brush with the melted butter right away.

Notes

  • Make ahead: shape the rolls, cover and refrigerate overnight. Let them rise at room temperature for about an hour, then bake.
  • Baked rolls freeze for 3 months; warm them wrapped in foil.
