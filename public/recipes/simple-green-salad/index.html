Simple Green Salad with Vinaigrette

Crisp mixed greens with a classic shallot and Dijon vinaigrette. The five-minute salad that goes with everything.

Instructions

  1. In the bottom of a large salad bowl, stir the shallot, vinegar, mustard and salt and let it stand for 5 minutes to soften the shallot.

  2. Whisk in the olive oil until thick and emulsified, and add the pepper.

  3. Add the greens and toss gently with your hands just before serving, until every leaf is lightly coated.

Notes

  • Dry greens hold dressing; wet greens water it down. A salad spinner is worth it.
  • The vinaigrette keeps a week in the fridge; shake before using.
