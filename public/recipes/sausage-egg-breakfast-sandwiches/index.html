Sausage, Egg and Cheese English Muffins

Homemade breakfast sandwiches with seasoned sausage patties, a folded egg and melted cheddar on toasted English muffins.

Makes
4 sandwiches
Prep
10 min
Cook

Total

Instructions

  1. Divide the sausage into 4 pieces and press each into a thin patty a little wider than a muffin; they shrink as they cook.

  2. Cook the patties in the skillet over medium heat until browned and cooked through with no pink in the middle, 4 minutes per side. They should reach 160°F. Move to a plate.Cook the patties in the skillet over medium heat until browned and cooked through with no pink in the middle, 4 minutes per side. They should reach 71°C. Move to a plate.

  3. Toast the English muffins while the sausage cooks.

  4. Wipe out the skillet and melt the butter over medium-low heat. Beat the eggs with the salt, pour them in and cook without stirring until almost set, 2–3 minutes. Fold into quarters with a spatula and cut into 4 pieces.

  5. Top each patty with a slice of cheese and a piece of egg, then return them to the pan for 30 seconds to melt the cheese. Sandwich in the toasted muffins.

Notes

  • To freeze, wrap cooled sandwiches in foil or paper towels and freeze for up to 2 months. Microwave from frozen, wrapped in a paper towel, for 1½ to 2 minutes, turning once.
