Fattoush

A crunchy Lebanese bread salad of toasted pita, lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber and radishes with a tangy sumac and lemon dressing.

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Toss the pita pieces with 2 tbsp of the oil, half the sumac and a pinch of the salt, spread them on the baking sheet and bake until golden and crisp, 8–10 minutes.Heat the oven to 190°C. Toss the pita pieces with 2 tbsp of the oil, half the sumac and a pinch of the salt, spread them on the baking sheet and bake until golden and crisp, 8–10 minutes.

  2. Whisk the lemon juice, garlic, the rest of the oil, the rest of the sumac and the rest of the salt in a large bowl.

  3. Add the lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber, radishes, green onions, parsley and mint and toss with the dressing.

  4. Add the pita chips right before serving and toss once more so they stay mostly crisp.

Notes

  • Sumac is a tart, lemony spice found in Middle Eastern shops and many supermarkets. Extra lemon zest is a stand-in.
  • Some pita contains milk; check the label if you need it vegan.
