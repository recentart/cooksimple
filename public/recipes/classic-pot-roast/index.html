Classic Pot Roast

A beef chuck roast braised low and slow with onions, carrots and potatoes until fork-tender, with the pan juices made into gravy.

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 300°F. Pat the roast dry and season all over with the salt and pepper.Heat the oven to 150°C. Pat the roast dry and season all over with the salt and pepper.

  2. Heat the oil in the pot over medium-high heat and sear the roast until deeply browned on all sides, 10–12 minutes total. Move to a plate.

  3. Add the onions and cook for 4 minutes, then the garlic and tomato paste for 1 minute. Pour in the broth and Worcestershire, scraping up the browned bits.

  4. Return the roast, add the thyme and rosemary, cover and braise in the oven for 2 hours.

  5. Add the carrots and potatoes around the roast, cover and return to the oven until the meat pulls apart easily with a fork, 60–90 minutes.

  6. Move the meat and vegetables to a platter. Skim the fat from the juices, bring them to a simmer and whisk in the cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp cold water. Simmer until thickened, 1–2 minutes, and pour over the roast.Move the meat and vegetables to a platter. Skim the fat from the juices, bring them to a simmer and whisk in the cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp (30 mL) cold water. Simmer until thickened, 1–2 minutes, and pour over the roast.

Notes

  • If the roast isn’t tender yet, it needs more time, not less; tough connective tissue only softens with long, gentle cooking.
