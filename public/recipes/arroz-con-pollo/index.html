Arroz con Pollo

Chicken and golden rice cooked together in one pot with peppers, onion, tomato, peas and olives. A complete, comforting meal.

Equipment

  • Large Dutch oven or deep skillet with a lid

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken with 1 tsp of the salt and the cumin. Heat the oil in the pot over medium-high heat and brown the chicken skin side down until golden, 6–8 minutes. Turn and brown for 2 minutes, then set aside.

  2. Pour off all but 2 tbsp of the fat. Cook the onion and green pepper over medium heat until soft, 6 minutes. Add the garlic, paprika, turmeric and tomato paste and stir for 1 minute.

  3. Add the rice and stir until coated, 1 minute. Pour in the broth with the remaining salt and bring to a boil.

  4. Nestle the chicken into the rice skin side up. Cover tightly, lower the heat and cook without lifting the lid until the rice is tender and the chicken reads 175°F, 25–30 minutes.Nestle the chicken into the rice skin side up. Cover tightly, lower the heat and cook without lifting the lid until the rice is tender and the chicken reads 79°C, 25–30 minutes.

  5. Scatter the peas and olives over the top, cover again and let stand off the heat for 10 minutes. Fluff the rice, scatter with cilantro and serve.

Notes

  • A tight lid is essential; if yours is loose, lay a sheet of foil under it.
