Tadka Dal

Creamy red lentils simmered with turmeric and finished with a sizzling tempering of cumin, garlic, chili and tomato in ghee or oil.

Instructions

  1. Bring the lentils, water and turmeric to a boil in the pot, skimming off any foam. Lower the heat and simmer, partly covered and stirring now and then, until the lentils collapse into a thick, creamy soup, 20–25 minutes. Stir in the salt.

  2. For the tadka, heat the oil in the small skillet over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds and chiles and let them sizzle for 30 seconds.

  3. Add the onion and cook until golden, 5 minutes. Add the garlic and ginger and cook until the garlic is light golden, 1 minute. Add the tomato and cook until soft, 2–3 minutes.

  4. Pour the sizzling tadka over the dal, stir in the garam masala and lemon juice, and scatter with cilantro. Serve with rice or flatbread.

Notes

  • Dal thickens as it cools; add hot water to loosen it before serving leftovers.
  • Using ghee makes it vegetarian rather than vegan.
