Pico de Gallo

A fresh, crunchy salsa of chopped tomato, onion, jalapeño, cilantro and lime. Great with chips, tacos, eggs and grilled meat.

Instructions

  1. Core and dice the tomatoes into small, even pieces. Finely chop the onion and seed and mince the jalapeño.

  2. Mix everything with the cilantro, lime juice and salt.

  3. Let it stand for 15 minutes so the flavors mingle, then stir and taste for salt. Serve with a slotted spoon.

Notes

  • Rinsing the chopped onion in cold water tames its bite.
  • Best the day it’s made; it turns watery as it sits.
