Creamy Coleslaw

Crunchy shredded cabbage and carrots in a tangy, lightly sweet mayonnaise and cider vinegar dressing. Perfect with barbecue.

Instructions

  1. Whisk the mayonnaise, vinegar, sugar, mustard, salt and pepper in a large bowl.

  2. Add the cabbage and carrots and toss until evenly coated.

  3. Chill for at least 30 minutes so the cabbage softens slightly and the flavors blend. Toss again before serving.

Notes

  • A bag of coleslaw mix saves all the shredding.
  • Keeps 2 days in the fridge; it gets softer and more watery the longer it sits.
