Easy Chocolate Mousse

A rich, airy chocolate mousse made by folding whipped cream into melted dark chocolate. No raw eggs and just four ingredients.

Instructions

  1. Warm ½ cup of the cream until steaming and pour it over the chocolate in the heatproof bowl. Let it sit for 2 minutes, then stir until smooth. Cool to lukewarm, 10 minutes.Warm 118 mL of the cream until steaming and pour it over the chocolate in the heatproof bowl. Let it sit for 2 minutes, then stir until smooth. Cool to lukewarm, 10 minutes.

  2. Whip the remaining cold cream with the sugar, vanilla and salt to soft peaks that just hold their shape.

  3. Stir a third of the whipped cream into the chocolate to lighten it, then gently fold in the rest until no streaks remain.

  4. Spoon into glasses and chill until set, at least 2 hours.

Notes

  • If the chocolate is too warm it will melt the cream; if too cold it will seize into flecks. Lukewarm is just right.
  • Keeps 3 days in the fridge.
