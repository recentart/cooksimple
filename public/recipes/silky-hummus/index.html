Silky Hummus

Ultra-smooth hummus made from canned chickpeas, tahini, lemon and garlic, whipped with ice water until light and creamy.

Instructions

  1. Blend the lemon juice, garlic and salt in the food processor, then let them sit for 5 minutes to mellow the garlic.

  2. Add the tahini and blend until thick and creamy. With the machine running, add the ice water a spoonful at a time until pale and smooth.

  3. Add the chickpeas and cumin and blend for a full 3 minutes, scraping down once, until very smooth. Add more ice water if it’s too thick.

  4. Spread in a shallow bowl, make a swirl with the back of a spoon, drizzle with oil and dust with paprika.

Notes

  • Blending the tahini with lemon and water first, before the chickpeas, is what makes it so smooth.
  • Keeps 5 days in the fridge.
