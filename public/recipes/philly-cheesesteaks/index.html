Philly Cheesesteaks

Thinly sliced beef seared on a hot skillet with onions and peppers, melted with provolone and piled into toasted rolls.

Makes
4 sandwiches
Prep
20 min
Cook
15 min
Total
35 min

  • Lunch
  • Egg-free
  • Nut-free
  • American

Sandwiches
Units

Equipment

  • Large cast-iron skillet or griddle

Instructions

  1. Freeze the beef for 20 minutes so it slices paper-thin, then slice it and chop it roughly.

  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in the skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion and pepper with a pinch of the salt until soft and golden, 8–10 minutes. Push them to one side.

  3. Turn the heat to high, add the rest of the oil and the beef in a thin layer, and season with the salt and pepper. Cook, chopping and turning with a spatula, until just browned, 2–3 minutes.

  4. Mix in the onions and divide into 4 piles the size of your rolls. Lay 2 slices of cheese over each and let them melt, 1 minute.

  5. Lay an open roll over each pile, slide the spatula underneath and flip everything into the roll. Serve hot.

Notes

  • Some shops sell pre-sliced beef for cheesesteaks or hot pot, which saves the slicing step.
