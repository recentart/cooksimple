Garlic Butter Shrimp

Plump shrimp seared in a hot skillet and tossed in garlicky lemon butter with parsley. Fifteen minutes, one pan.

Instructions

  1. Pat the shrimp very dry and season with the salt and pepper.

  2. Heat the oil in the skillet over medium-high heat. Add the shrimp in a single layer and cook without moving until pink underneath, 1–2 minutes. Flip and cook 1 minute more. Move to a plate.

  3. Lower the heat to medium and add the butter, garlic and pepper flakes. Stir until the garlic is fragrant but not browned, 1 minute.

  4. Return the shrimp, add the lemon juice and toss until the shrimp are opaque and glossy with sauce, 1 minute. Scatter with parsley and serve with rice, pasta or crusty bread.

Notes

  • Frozen shrimp are fine; thaw them overnight in the fridge or in a bowl of cold water for 15 minutes.
