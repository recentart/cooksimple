Vegetable Barley Soup

A hearty, chewy barley soup loaded with carrots, celery, mushrooms, tomatoes and green beans in a savory vegetable broth.

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in the pot over medium heat. Cook the onion, carrots and celery until softened, 8 minutes.

  2. Add the mushrooms and cook until they release their liquid and it evaporates, 6–8 minutes. Stir in the garlic and tomato paste for 1 minute.

  3. Add the barley, tomatoes, broth, thyme, bay leaf and salt. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer, partly covered, until the barley is chewy-tender, 35–40 minutes.

  4. Add the green beans for the last 8 minutes of cooking. Remove the bay leaf, taste for salt and stir in the parsley.

Notes

  • Barley keeps soaking up broth as the soup sits. Add water or broth when reheating.
  • Keeps 5 days in the fridge and freezes for 3 months.
