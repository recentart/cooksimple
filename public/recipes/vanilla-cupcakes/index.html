Vanilla Cupcakes

Light, tender vanilla cupcakes with a fluffy vanilla buttercream. Simple, reliable, and easy to decorate for any occasion.

Equipment

  • 12-cup muffin tin
  • Paper liners
  • Hand or stand mixer

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and line the muffin tin. Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt.Heat the oven to 175°C and line the muffin tin. Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt.

  2. Beat the butter and sugar until pale and fluffy, 3 minutes. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.

  3. On low speed, add the flour mixture in three parts, alternating with the milk, mixing just until smooth.

  4. Fill the liners two-thirds full and bake until the tops spring back and a toothpick comes out clean, 18–20 minutes. Cool completely on a rack.

  5. Beat the butter until creamy, then beat in the powdered sugar, cream, vanilla and salt until light and fluffy, 3 minutes. Pipe or spread onto the cooled cupcakes.

Notes

  • Room-temperature eggs and milk blend smoothly into the butter; cold ones can make the batter look curdled.
