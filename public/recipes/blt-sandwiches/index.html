BLT Sandwiches

Crisp oven-baked bacon, ripe tomato and crunchy lettuce on toasted bread with mayonnaise. Simple, but only as good as the tomatoes.

Makes
4 sandwiches
Prep
10 min
Cook
20 min
Total
30 min
Oven
400°F205°C

Equipment

  • Rimmed baking sheet
  • Foil or parchment

Instructions

  1. Line the baking sheet with foil or parchment and lay the bacon on it in one layer. Put it in a cold oven, then set the oven to 400°F.Line the baking sheet with foil or parchment and lay the bacon on it in one layer. Put it in a cold oven, then set the oven to 205°C.

  2. Bake until the bacon is browned and crisp, 17–20 minutes from turning on the oven. Drain on paper towels.

  3. Toast the bread and spread each slice with mayonnaise.

  4. Sprinkle the tomato slices with the salt and pepper. Layer lettuce, tomatoes and three slices of bacon on half the toast, then close the sandwiches and cut in half.

Notes

  • Baking bacon means no spatter and flat, evenly crisp strips. Let the fat cool before throwing it away; never pour it down the drain.
