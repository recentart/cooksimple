Italian Pasta Salad

Tangy pasta salad with cherry tomatoes, cucumber, olives, mozzarella and red onion in a garlicky red wine vinaigrette.

Equipment

  • Large pot
  • Colander

Instructions

  1. Bring the water to a boil with the salt. Cook the pasta until tender, 10–12 minutes, a minute longer than al dente since it firms up as it cools. Drain and rinse under cold water until cool.

  2. Whisk the vinegar, olive oil, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper in a large bowl.

  3. Add the drained pasta and toss well so it soaks up the dressing.

  4. Fold in the tomatoes, cucumber, olives, red onion, mozzarella and basil. Taste and adjust the salt and vinegar. Chill for at least 30 minutes if you have time.

Notes

  • The pasta absorbs dressing as it sits. Keep a couple of spoonfuls back and toss them in just before serving.
  • Keeps in the fridge for 3 days.
