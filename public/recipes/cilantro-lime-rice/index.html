Cilantro Lime Rice

Fluffy long-grain rice tossed with fresh cilantro, lime zest and juice. The classic side for burritos, tacos and bowls.

Equipment

Instructions

  1. Bring the rice, water, oil and salt to a boil in the saucepan.

  2. Cover, turn the heat to low and simmer until the water is absorbed, 18 minutes. Take off the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes.

  3. Fluff with a fork and fold in the lime zest, juice and cilantro.

Notes

  • Add the lime and cilantro after cooking; heat dulls their fresh flavor.
