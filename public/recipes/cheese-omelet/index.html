Cheese Omelet

A tender, golden French-style omelet folded around melted cheddar. One pan, three eggs and about ten minutes.

Equipment

  • 8-inch nonstick skillet20 cm nonstick skillet
  • Silicone spatula

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs and salt with a fork until smooth and a little frothy.

  2. Melt the butter in the skillet over medium heat and swirl it to coat. When the foam subsides, pour in the eggs.

  3. Stir quickly with the spatula while shaking the pan for about 20 seconds, until small curds form. Then stop stirring and spread the eggs into an even layer.

  4. Cook without stirring until the bottom is set and the top is just barely moist, 1–2 minutes. Scatter the cheese over one half.

  5. Fold the other half over the cheese, let it sit for 30 seconds to melt, then slide the omelet onto a plate. Sprinkle with chives.

Notes

  • Warm fillings such as sautéed mushrooms or spinach can go in with the cheese; keep them to about a quarter cup so the omelet folds neatly.
