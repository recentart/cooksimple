Cottage Pie

Savory ground beef and vegetables in a rich gravy under a fluffy, golden mashed potato topping. British comfort food at its best.

Instructions

  1. Cover the potatoes with cold salted water, bring to a boil and cook until very tender, 15–18 minutes. Drain, return to the pot and mash with the butter, warm milk and salt until smooth.

  2. Meanwhile heat the oven to 400°F. Heat the oil in the skillet over medium-high heat and brown the beef, breaking it up, 6–8 minutes. Spoon off excess fat.Meanwhile heat the oven to 205°C. Heat the oil in the skillet over medium-high heat and brown the beef, breaking it up, 6–8 minutes. Spoon off excess fat.

  3. Add the onion and carrots and cook until softened, 6 minutes. Stir in the garlic, flour and tomato paste for 1 minute.

  4. Stir in the broth, Worcestershire, thyme and salt and simmer until thick and gravy-like, 8–10 minutes. Stir in the peas and spread the filling in the baking dish.

  5. Spoon the mash over the top, spread it to the edges and rough up the surface with a fork. Sprinkle with cheese, if using.

  6. Bake until the filling bubbles at the edges and the top is golden, 20–25 minutes. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

Notes

  • Made with ground lamb instead of beef, it becomes shepherd’s pie.
  • Assemble ahead and refrigerate for up to 2 days, or freeze for 3 months. Bake from the fridge, adding 15 minutes.
