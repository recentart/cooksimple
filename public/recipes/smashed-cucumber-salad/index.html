Smashed Cucumber Salad

Cucumbers smashed and tossed with garlic, rice vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil and chili. Crunchy, tangy and ready in 15 minutes.

Instructions

  1. Lay the cucumbers on a board and whack them firmly along their length with the flat of a big knife or a rolling pin until they split. Cut into bite-size chunks.

  2. Toss the cucumbers with the salt and sugar in a colander and let them drain for 10 minutes.

  3. Mix the garlic, vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil and pepper flakes in a bowl.

  4. Shake off the cucumber liquid, toss the cucumbers in the dressing and sprinkle with sesame seeds. Serve soon, while still crunchy.

Notes

  • Smashing makes rough edges that soak up more dressing than neat slices.
