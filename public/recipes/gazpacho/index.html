Gazpacho

Chilled Spanish tomato soup blended with cucumber, pepper, garlic, sherry vinegar and olive oil. No cooking, just ripe summer tomatoes.

Servings
4
Prep
15 min
Total
2 hr 15 min

Vegan tip: check the labels on day-old crusty bread (some breads contain milk or eggs).

Instructions

  1. Put the tomatoes, cucumber, pepper, onion, garlic, bread (if using), vinegar and salt in the blender.

  2. Blend until completely smooth, about 1 minute. With the blender running, pour in the oil in a thin stream; the soup will turn a lighter orange-red and creamy.

  3. Taste and adjust the salt and vinegar. For a silkier soup, press it through a sieve.

  4. Chill for at least 2 hours; gazpacho needs to be really cold. Serve in bowls or glasses with a drizzle of olive oil and finely diced cucumber on top.

Notes

  • It is only as good as the tomatoes, so make it in summer when they are ripe and sweet.
  • Leave out the bread for a gluten-free soup. Keeps 3 days in the fridge.
