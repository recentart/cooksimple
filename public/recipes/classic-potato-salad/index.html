Classic Potato Salad

Tender potatoes tossed while warm with a little vinegar, then folded into a creamy dressing with celery, eggs and herbs.

Instructions

  1. Cover the potatoes with cold, well-salted water, bring to a boil and simmer until just tender when pierced, 10–12 minutes. Don’t let them fall apart.

  2. Drain and spread on a tray, then sprinkle with the vinegar while hot; warm potatoes soak up the flavor. Cool to room temperature, 20 minutes.

  3. Stir the mayonnaise, mustard, salt and pepper together in a large bowl.

  4. Fold in the potatoes, eggs, celery, onion and dill. Chill for at least 1 hour before serving.

Notes

  • Keep mayonnaise-based salads cold. Don’t leave them out for more than 2 hours (1 hour on a hot day).
  • Keeps 3 days in the fridge.
