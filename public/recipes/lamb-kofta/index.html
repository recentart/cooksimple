Lamb Kofta

Spiced ground lamb shaped on skewers with onion, garlic, parsley, cumin and cinnamon, grilled or broiled and served with yogurt sauce.

Servings
4
Prep

Cook

Instructions

  1. Mix the lamb, onion, garlic, parsley, cumin, coriander, paprika, cinnamon and salt with your hands until well combined and slightly sticky.

  2. Divide into 8 portions and shape each around a skewer into a log about 5-inch long. Chill for 15 minutes to firm up.Divide into 8 portions and shape each around a skewer into a log about 13 cm long. Chill for 15 minutes to firm up.

  3. Stir the yogurt, lemon juice and mint with a pinch of salt.

  4. Heat the grill or broiler to high. Cook the kofta, turning a few times, until browned all over and they reach 160°F, 8–10 minutes.Heat the grill or broiler to high. Cook the kofta, turning a few times, until browned all over and they reach 71°C, 8–10 minutes.

  5. Serve with the yogurt sauce, warm pitas and a chopped tomato and cucumber salad.

Notes

  • No skewers? Shape the mixture into small patties and cook them in a skillet.
