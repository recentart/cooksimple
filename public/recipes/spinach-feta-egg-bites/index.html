Spinach Feta Egg Bites

Baked egg muffins with spinach, red pepper and feta. Make a tray on Sunday and reheat a couple each morning in under a minute.

Equipment

  • 12-cup muffin tin

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F with a rack in the middle. Brush the muffin cups generously with oil, or use silicone liners.Heat the oven to 175°C with a rack in the middle. Brush the muffin cups generously with oil, or use silicone liners.

  2. Heat the oil in the skillet over medium heat. Cook the bell pepper until soft, 4 minutes, then add the spinach and stir until wilted, 1–2 minutes. Let it cool a little.

  3. Whisk the eggs, milk, salt and pepper in a large measuring jug or bowl until smooth.

  4. Divide the vegetables and feta among the muffin cups, then pour the egg mixture over them, filling each cup about three-quarters full.

  5. Bake until puffed and set in the center, 20–24 minutes. Cool in the tin for 5 minutes, then run a thin knife around each one and lift it out.

Notes

  • Refrigerate in a covered container for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 2 months. Reheat in the microwave for 30 to 60 seconds.
  • The bites puff in the oven and settle as they cool; that’s expected.
