Rice Pilaf

Fluffy long-grain rice toasted in butter with toasted pasta and onion, then simmered in broth. Every grain stays separate.

Servings
4
Prep
5 min
Cook

Equipment

  • Medium saucepan with a tight lid

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter in the saucepan over medium heat. Add the pasta and stir until golden brown, 2–3 minutes.

  2. Add the onion and cook until soft, 3 minutes. Add the rice and stir until the grains look translucent at the edges, 2 minutes.

  3. Pour in the broth and salt and bring to a boil. Cover tightly, turn the heat to low and simmer until the liquid is absorbed, 18 minutes.

  4. Take the pan off the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork and stir in the parsley.

Notes

  • Use vegetable broth to make it vegetarian.
