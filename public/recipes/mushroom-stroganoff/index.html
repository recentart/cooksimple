Mushroom Stroganoff

A meaty-tasting vegetarian stroganoff of browned mushrooms and onions in a creamy sour cream and mustard sauce over noodles.

Instructions

  1. Start a pot of salted water for the noodles. Heat the butter and oil in the skillet over medium-high heat.

  2. Add the mushrooms and cook without stirring until browned underneath, 4 minutes. Add the salt and cook, stirring, until their liquid evaporates and they’re well browned, 6–8 minutes.

  3. Add the onion and cook until soft, 5 minutes. Stir in the garlic, paprika and flour for 1 minute.

  4. Stir in the broth, soy sauce and mustard and simmer until thickened, 4 minutes. Meanwhile cook the noodles and drain.

  5. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the sour cream. Warm gently without boiling. Serve over the noodles with parsley.

Notes

  • A mix of mushrooms, such as cremini, shiitake and oyster, gives the best flavor.
