Crispy Tofu Stir-Fry

Cornstarch-crisped tofu tossed with broccoli, bell pepper and snap peas in a sticky garlic-ginger soy sauce. Vegan and quick.

Equipment

  • Large nonstick skillet or wok

Instructions

  1. Pat the tofu cubes very dry and toss them with the cornstarch until coated.

  2. Heat 2 tbsp oil in the skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the tofu, turning every couple of minutes, until golden and crisp on most sides, 8–10 minutes. Move to a plate.

  3. Stir the soy sauce, maple syrup, vinegar, 1 tsp cornstarch and ¼ cup water in a small bowl.Stir the soy sauce, maple syrup, vinegar, 1 tsp cornstarch and 59 mL water in a small bowl.

  4. Add the rest of the oil and the broccoli with a splash of water and stir-fry for 2 minutes. Add the pepper and snap peas for 2 minutes, then the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.

  5. Pour in the sauce and stir until it thickens, 1 minute. Return the tofu, toss to coat, and finish with sesame oil. Serve with rice.

Notes

  • Press the tofu between towels under a heavy pan for 15 minutes for the crispest result.
